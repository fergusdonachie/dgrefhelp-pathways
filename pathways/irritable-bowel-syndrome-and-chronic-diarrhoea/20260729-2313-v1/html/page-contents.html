Irritable bowel syndrome and chronic diarrhoea

This pathway provides a single primary-care approach to persistent loose stool and suspected IBS. It supports a positive diagnosis of IBS while identifying patients who need investigation or referral for cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, coeliac disease, infection or malabsorption.

Two key testing messages

  • Include HIV serology in the initial GP blood panel for chronic diarrhoea.
  • Do not request faecal calprotectin routinely for every possible IBS presentation. Use it selectively after a negative qFIT when diarrhoea persists and inflammatory bowel disease remains a realistic differential.
