# IBS and chronic diarrhoea - concise combined pathway review pack

**Run:** 20260803-1525-v2

**Audience:** NHS Dumfries & Galloway GPs, ANPs and community clinicians

**Status:** Draft for local clinical/editorial review

**Date:** 3 August 2026

## Main revision

The combined pathway has been shortened and reorganised into the requested clinical sequence:

1. Primary care assessment
2. Primary care management
3. Who to refer and how urgently
4. Who not to refer

References remain as a final non-clinical accordion.

## Cancer content now included directly

The referral section now reproduces the relevant Scottish 2025 thresholds rather than directing readers elsewhere:

- qFIT indications and colorectal USC referral at ≥20 micrograms Hb/g faeces;
- direct colorectal USC triggers where qFIT is not appropriate;
- ovarian symptoms requiring CA125 plus USC pelvic ultrasound;
- ovarian USC referral for suspicious imaging/mass, CA125 ≥35 IU/mL or unexplained ascites.

## Local messages retained

- HIV serology is part of the initial GP investigation of chronic diarrhoea.
- Faecal calprotectin is selective, not a routine IBS test.
- IBS management remains detailed enough for primary care.
