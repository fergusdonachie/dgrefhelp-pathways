Irritable bowel syndrome and chronic diarrhoea

Use this pathway for adults with persistent loose stool or suspected IBS. Exclude cancer, inflammation, infection, coeliac disease, medicine effects and malabsorption, then make a positive diagnosis of IBS where appropriate.

Key testing messages

  • Include HIV serology in the initial GP investigation of chronic diarrhoea.
  • Do not request faecal calprotectin routinely for possible IBS. Use it for persistent loose stool when qFIT is negative and inflammatory bowel disease remains a realistic differential.
