Elbow pain in adults: assessment, management and referral

Most elbow pain is self-limiting tendinopathy managed in primary care. The key decisions are spotting the hot elbow that needs same-day discussion, and the ulnar nerve lesion that needs timely referral.

  • Lateral/medial epicondylitis: self-limiting in most within 12 months — education, load modification, exercise. Avoid corticosteroid injection — worse long-term outcomes.
  • Olecranon bursitis: usually conservative; hot/red with fever → same-day discussion.
  • Cubital tunnel: night splinting/positioning first; motor weakness or wasting → refer with nerve conduction studies.
