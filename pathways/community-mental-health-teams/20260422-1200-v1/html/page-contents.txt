Community Mental Health Teams (CMHTs) provide secondary care mental health assessment and support for people aged 18 and over in Dumfries & Galloway. There are five locality teams (Wigtownshire, Stewartry, Dumfries, Nithsdale, Annandale & Eskdale), each with adult and older adult sub-teams covering both functional and organic presentations.
