Neuropathic pain management

Use this pathway for adults with clinically suspected or confirmed neuropathic pain managed in non-specialist care. Identify and treat the underlying cause, agree functional goals and use time-limited medicine trials with planned review.

Key prescribing principles

  • In NHS Dumfries & Galloway, the preferred formulary sequence is amitriptyline, then gabapentin, then duloxetine or pregabalin. Move to the next option when a medicine is contraindicated, not tolerated or ineffective after an adequate trial.
  • Start low, titrate gradually and change only one medicine at a time. Review pain, function, sleep, adverse effects and continued need.
  • Gabapentin and pregabalin require creatinine-clearance-based dose adjustment and carry risks of sedation, respiratory depression, dependence and withdrawal.
  • This pathway does not apply to sciatica, chronic primary pain, cancer/palliative pain or people under 18. Trigeminal neuralgia is managed separately within this pathway.
