# Neuropathic pain management - review pack

**Run:** 20260810-1301-v1

**Audience:** NHS Dumfries & Galloway GPs, ANPs, pharmacists and community clinicians

**Status:** Draft for local clinical, pharmacy and editorial review

**Date:** 10 August 2026

## Purpose

This draft replaces the existing DGRefHelp neuropathic pain page, which was last reviewed in August 2022 and is marked as under review. It incorporates the supplied Pain Team draft, the 2025 NHS D&G formulary, NICE guidance and current MHRA/SPS safety advice.

## Main pathway structure

1. Primary care assessment and exclusions
2. Treatment principles and self-management
3. Local formulary sequence with practical titration
4. Localised pain and trigeminal neuralgia
5. Renal adjustment, switching and withdrawal
6. Urgent, condition-specific and pain-service referral thresholds
7. Who can remain in primary care

## Material changes from the supplied draft

- The local formulary order is retained: amitriptyline, gabapentin, then duloxetine or pregabalin. The text also recognises the NICE requirement to select and switch according to contraindications, tolerability and patient factors.
- Amitriptyline is presented with the licensed neuropathic-pain range of 25-75 mg daily. The supplied draft's advice about routine doses above 100 mg is not reproduced.
- Duloxetine is presented as 60 mg once daily for diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain, with 30 mg daily as a tolerability step where clinically appropriate. Doses above 60 mg require an individual risk-benefit review.
- Gabapentinoid content now includes the January 2026 MHRA warning on addiction, dependence, tolerance and withdrawal, plus respiratory-depression risk with opioids and other CNS depressants.
- Fixed withdrawal schedules are labelled as practical starting examples. Tapering must be individualised and may take weeks or months.
- Sciatica and chronic primary pain are explicitly excluded because their NICE pharmacological recommendations differ.
- Long-term tramadol, morphine and other non-recommended agents are excluded from non-specialist initiation. Lidocaine plasters and capsaicin 8% patches are reserved for licensed/specialist use.
