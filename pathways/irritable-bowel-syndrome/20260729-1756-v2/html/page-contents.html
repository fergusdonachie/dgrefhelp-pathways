Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)

IBS is a disorder of gut-brain interaction. Make a positive diagnosis when symptoms meet Rome IV criteria, alarm features and relevant alternative diagnoses have been assessed, and first-line investigations are satisfactory.

Faecal calprotectin is not a routine test for every patient with possible IBS

Request it selectively when qFIT is negative, diarrhoea or loose stools persist, and inflammatory bowel disease remains a realistic differential. Do not use faecal calprotectin to investigate possible colorectal cancer.

This draft incorporates the NHS Scotland CfSD/Modernising Patient Pathways Programme IBS pathway published in January 2025.
