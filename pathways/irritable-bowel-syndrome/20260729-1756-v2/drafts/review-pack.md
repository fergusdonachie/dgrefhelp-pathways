# Irritable bowel syndrome - review pack

**Run:** 20260729-1756-v2

**Audience:** NHS Dumfries & Galloway GPs, ANPs and community clinicians

**Status:** Draft for local clinical/editorial review

**Date:** 29 July 2026

## Purpose of this update

- Align the DGRefHelp draft with the January 2025 NHS Scotland CfSD/MPPP IBS pathway.
- Respond to the local microbiology audit showing a substantial rise in primary-care faecal calprotectin requests.
- Replace the previous instruction to request faecal calprotectin for every possible IBS presentation.

## Main clinical change

Faecal calprotectin is now described as a selective test: persistent diarrhoea/loose stool, negative qFIT, no suspected colorectal cancer, infection considered, and IBD still a realistic differential. It is not a routine test for IBS-C, stable established IBS or every patient meeting Rome IV criteria.

## Other changes from v1

- Removed the blanket “age over 45” alarm rule.
- Added the national pathway instruction to consider ovarian cancer in women aged 50 or over with new IBS-type symptoms in the last 12 months.
- Simplified drug advice to current NICE-supported steps.
- Removed ondansetron and prucalopride from the primary-care draft.
- Prioritised specialist AHP/dietetic input before medical gastroenterology for persistent but non-severe symptoms.
