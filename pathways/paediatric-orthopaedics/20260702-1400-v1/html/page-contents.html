Paediatric orthopaedics: the limping child, normal variants and common presentations

Two jobs: never miss the septic joint, SUFE or malignancy in the limping child — and confidently reassure the large group of normal developmental variants that need no referral.

  • Limping child with fever, non-weight-bearing, or age <3 → same-day paediatric/orthopaedic assessment.
  • Adolescent with hip or knee pain: think SUFE — urgent, keep non-weight-bearing until excluded.
  • Normal variants (flexible flat feet, in-toeing, bow legs, knock knees, curly toes) — reassure when symmetrical, painless and within age norms.
