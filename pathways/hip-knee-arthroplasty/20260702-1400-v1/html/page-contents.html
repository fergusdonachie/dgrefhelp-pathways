Hip and knee osteoarthritis: referral for consideration of joint replacement

Refer when osteoarthritis substantially affects quality of life despite core non-surgical treatment, and the patient is willing to consider surgery. Diagnosis is clinical; imaging confirms rather than makes the decision.

  • Before referral: core treatment tried (exercise, weight management, analgesia) and a recent weight-bearing X-ray.
  • Refer: ongoing pain (especially night/rest pain), substantial functional limitation, and patient willing to consider surgery.
  • Do not delay referral solely on the basis of BMI, age or smoking status (NICE NG226) — but flag optimisation opportunities in the referral.
  • Urgent: suspected avascular necrosis, rapidly progressive destruction, or previous hip surgery with new symptoms.
