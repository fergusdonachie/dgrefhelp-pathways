Safe withdrawal of prednisolone therapy in adults with non-endocrine disease

This guidance is relevant to primary care and outpatient settings.

Use for: adult, non-pregnant patients who have received prednisolone 5 mg daily or more for 4 weeks or longer for a non-endocrine condition.

The suggested taper of higher doses is based on the European Society of Endocrinology and Endocrine Society joint clinical guideline on glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency.
