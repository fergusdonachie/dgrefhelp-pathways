Safe withdrawal of long-term prednisolone in adults

Use this guide for adults taking prednisolone for a non-endocrine condition when the underlying disease is controlled and treatment is no longer required. It applies particularly after prednisolone 5 mg daily or more for 4 weeks or longer.

Key principles

  • Do not stop long-term prednisolone abruptly. Reduce higher doses more quickly, then use smaller reductions and longer intervals near the physiological dose of 4-6 mg daily.
  • Only start the final withdrawal when the condition being treated is controlled. A disease-specific or specialist taper takes precedence.
  • Routine adrenal testing is not required while the patient remains on a supraphysiological dose or still needs prednisolone for disease control.
  • At 5 mg daily or below, regard the patient as potentially adrenal-suppressed until recovery is demonstrated. Provide sick-day advice and ensure they have a Steroid Emergency Card.
