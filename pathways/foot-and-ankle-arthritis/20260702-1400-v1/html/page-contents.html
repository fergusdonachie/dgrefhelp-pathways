Foot and ankle arthritis (including hallux rigidus): management and surgical referral

Complements the existing foot & ankle pathways by covering the end-stage arthritis decisions: who benefits from orthopaedic referral for fusion, replacement or cheilectomy, and who is better served by footwear, orthotics and MSK services.

  • First line for all: footwear modification (stiff/rocker sole), activity modification, analgesia, orthotics via MSK/podiatry.
  • Refer: confirmed arthritis on weight-bearing X-ray with persistent significant pain/disability despite conservative care, in a patient willing to consider surgery.
  • Urgent: suspected Charcot foot (red, hot, swollen foot in diabetes/neuropathy) — same-day diabetes foot pathway, not routine orthopaedics.
