# Chronic diarrhoea investigation - review pack

**Run:** 20260729-1756-v1

**Audience:** NHS Dumfries & Galloway GPs, ANPs and community clinicians

**Status:** Draft for local clinical/editorial review

**Date:** 29 July 2026

## Purpose of this update

- Implement the local gastroenterology request to add HIV serology to the blood tests completed by GPs.
- Retain the useful structure of the existing DGRefHelp chronic diarrhoea page.
- Align use of faecal calprotectin with the January 2025 national IBS pathway and the local microbiology concern about excess requesting.

## Main clinical changes

1. HIV serology now appears in the initial GP blood panel rather than only under secondary immunodeficiency assessment.
2. qFIT is positioned before faecal calprotectin.
3. Faecal calprotectin is reserved for persistent diarrhoea with a negative qFIT where IBD remains a realistic differential.
4. The referral section asks for HIV and the completed first-line work-up to accompany a gastroenterology referral.

## Points to confirm before publication

1. Current D&G faecal calprotectin laboratory ranges and wording.
2. Exact local stool microbiology tests and any laboratory restrictions.
3. Current suspected colorectal cancer, gastroenterology and gastroenterology dietetic referral links.
4. Whether the “under 40” dietetic recommendation from the existing page remains current.
