Chronic diarrhoea investigation

Assess persistent loose stool systematically in primary care, looking for cancer, inflammation, infection, medication effects and malabsorption before referral.

Local update: include HIV serology in the initial GP blood panel

HIV testing should be completed in primary care alongside the other first-line blood tests rather than deferred to secondary assessment. Discuss the test and obtain consent in the usual way.

Use faecal calprotectin selectively after qFIT when inflammatory bowel disease remains a realistic differential; it is not a screening test for every patient with diarrhoea.
