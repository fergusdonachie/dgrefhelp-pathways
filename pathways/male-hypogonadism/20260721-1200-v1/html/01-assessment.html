Do not test during an acute, reversible illness or when symptoms are vague — testosterone falls transiently and the result will mislead. Test when the patient is well.

First test — total testosterone. Brown tube, taken 0800–0900h; reference range 10–30 nmol/L. Interpret against three bands:

<7 nmol/L — androgen deficiency likely

7–11 nmol/L — equivocal

>11 nmol/L — androgen deficiency unlikely. Referral not indicated; consider another cause for the symptoms.

For a result <7 or 7–11 nmol/L with no co-existing illness, repeat in 4 weeks — fasted, 0800–0900h — with bioavailable testosterone, LH/FSH, prolactin, TSH and ferritin. The confirmatory panel is the same for both bands; only the subsequent action differs (see Who to refer).
