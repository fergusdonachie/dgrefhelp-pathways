Refer to endocrinology

  • Confirmed testosterone <7 nmol/L — once illness and drug effects are excluded.
  • Confirmed 7–11 nmol/L — only where the patient is clearly symptomatic, not improving, and illness/drug effects are excluded.

Endocrinology accepts referrals for confirmed testosterone deficiency and will discuss treatment. In borderline cases a 3–6 month therapeutic trial may be considered after counselling on the potential benefits and risks.
