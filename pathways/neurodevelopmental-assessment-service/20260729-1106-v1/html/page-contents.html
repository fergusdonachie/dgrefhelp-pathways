Neurodevelopmental Assessment Service (NDAS)

NDAS provides diagnostic assessment for children and young people with significant neurodevelopmental differences. It is not a support or treatment service.

Key principle: refer to NDAS only when the child or young person and their family request diagnostic assessment. A formal diagnosis is not required to access support, and support should not wait for an NDAS assessment.

  • Referral is available up to the child's or young person's 18th birthday when all referral criteria are met.
  • The referral route is family self-referral using the NDAS Microsoft Form.
  • There is no need for a separate professional referral. Letters and SCI Gateway referrals are accepted, but the family will be directed to complete the same Microsoft Form.
  • NDAS aims to review the screening information and share a decision with the family within 4 weeks of receiving it.
