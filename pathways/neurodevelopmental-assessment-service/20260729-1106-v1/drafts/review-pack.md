## Key message

NDAS is a diagnostic assessment service, not a support or treatment service. Refer only when the child or young person and their family request diagnostic assessment. A diagnosis is not required to access support, and support should not wait for assessment.

## Referral threshold

A child or young person can be referred up to their 18th birthday when all of the following apply:

- The child or young person is developing differently.
- The differences may be due to a neurodevelopmental disorder.
- There is evidence of significant impact on personal, family, social, educational, occupational or another important area of functioning.
- The child or young person and family request diagnostic assessment.

## Information required for screening

- Child's or young person's details
- Consent to begin the assessment process
- Family information explaining why diagnostic assessment is requested
- Developmental history
- Information about the impact of the differences

## Referral routes

- **Family self-referral:** [NDAS Microsoft Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/b2C5h4X86M)
- **Professional referral:** letter or Request for Assistance (RFA) form. NDAS then provides access to the screening forms to the family.

## What happens next

NDAS aims to review the screening information and share a decision with the family within 4 weeks of receipt.

- If diagnostic assessment is recommended, an acknowledgement letter is sent and the child or young person is added to the waiting list.
- If diagnostic assessment is not recommended, a letter explains the decision and recommends other services that may be more appropriate.
