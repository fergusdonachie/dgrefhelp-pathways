## What changed from the source document

### Added (the gap identified)
A new **"Making the diagnosis in primary care"** section — the source doc told GPs a diagnosis was required before referral but not how to make one. The new section is a 3-step process:

1. **History/examination** — fatigue pattern, PEM, sleep, cognition, mimic screen
2. **One shared blood panel** — the exclusion workup is essentially the same for all three conditions, so it is presented once (FBC, U&E, LFT, TFT, CRP/PV, Ca/PO4, HbA1c, ferritin, coeliac, urinalysis; +CK if pain)
3. **Condition-specific criteria** in a 3-column grid, super-simplified:
   - **ME/CFS**: all 4 of fatigue/PEM/unrefreshing sleep/cognitive difficulties; suspect at 6 weeks, diagnose at 3 months (NG206)
   - **Long COVID**: >12 weeks post-infection not otherwise explained = post-COVID syndrome; sit-to-stand sats, lying/standing BP, CXR by 12 weeks if respiratory (NG188)
   - **Fibromyalgia**: pain ≥4/5 regions ≥3 months; RCP worksheet WPI ≥7 + SSS ≥5 (or WPI 4–6 + SSS ≥9)

Plus a red-flag box (features suggesting an alternative diagnosis) and a confidence-building blue box ("GPs can and should make these diagnoses").
