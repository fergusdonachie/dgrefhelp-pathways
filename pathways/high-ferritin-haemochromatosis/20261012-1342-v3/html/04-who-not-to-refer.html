• Raised ferritin with Tsat ≤45% — iron overload unlikely; manage the underlying cause.
  • C282Y homozygotes without biochemical iron overload — annual ferritin and Tsat in primary care until 65.
  • C282Y/H63D compound heterozygotes with ferritin <1000 ng/mL — primary care management.
  • H63D homozygotes, carriers of one mutation, and non-carriers — not at risk of HFE iron overload.
  • Raised ferritin with a clear alternative explanation (alcohol excess, metabolic syndrome / MASLD, inflammation) — manage the cause.
  • Ferritin tested during acute illness or within 6 weeks of it — repeat when well.
  • Family members for testing only — the GP requests ferritin, Tsat and HFE genotype.
