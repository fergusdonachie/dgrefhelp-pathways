# Eating disorders in adults review pack

Version 20261005-v3 · 5 October 2026 · Draft for local review

## Outcome

Updated the existing five-section DGRefHelp draft and prepared a separate LES update review. The page is for adults aged 18 and over. It covers assessment, primary care management, who to refer, who not to refer and references. Local referral arrangements and clinical ownership must be confirmed before publication.

The LES requires substantive updating, but the supplied 2008 copy cannot establish current commissioning or payment status. This corrects the earlier review pack's categorical claim that it was no longer a contractual standard. Do not cease an existing service or treat its current funding as invalid on this evidence alone.

## Changes from the existing draft

- Added an explicit draft banner and adult scope.
- Removed SCI Gateway as an asserted route: it was not substantiated by the supplied attachments. Retained the documented CMHT/SEDS service model and GP dietetic access with referral-method confirmation required.
- Clarified BMI calculation, ARFID recognition and that investigations must not delay urgent referral.
- Made safety-netting, next review, monitoring ownership, result action and transfer arrangements more concrete.
- Checked the current MEED PDF, whose cover records an update in December 2025. Distinguished its Table 1 traffic-light indicators from the frontline checklist: the former includes intake below 500 kcal/day for two or more days; the latter uses below 400 kcal/day as a prompt. The page now uses the Table 1 wording when describing red indicators.
- Added examples of biochemical checklist triggers without mislabelling them as Table 1 red thresholds. Retained the full-framework link and clinical context.
- Qualified bone-density advice as applying to adults with anorexia nervosa.
- Added the LES comparison, conflicting monitoring schedules, visually verified inverted BMI formula and proposed replacement clauses.

## Sources and limits

All four supplied documents were extracted and reviewed. The ICP was additionally converted to PDF to inspect the document-control page and its otherwise missing flowchart. Relevant LES pages were visually checked, including the monitoring table and both BMI formulae. The original files were not edited.

National checks used the [Scottish specification](https://www.gov.scot/publications/national-specification-care-treatment-eating-disorders-scotland/), particularly outcomes 3, 5 and 7, [MEED CR233](https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/improving-care/campaigning-for-better-mental-health-policy/college-reports/2022-college-reports/cr233), Table 1 and Appendix 3, and [NICE NG69](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng69/chapter/recommendations). The NICE website blocked direct full-page retrieval; relevant recommendations were available through indexed official content and the existing source-grounded draft. Direct SIGN retrieval was unavailable; SIGN 164 remains a background reference and no new recommendation relies on an unverified change to SIGN.

The Copilot summary is subordinate to the full ICP. Neither attachment instructions nor service descriptions constitute an instruction to publish or change contractual obligations.
